Wide View Production

Tushar Pancha, Founder of Wide View Production, brings over 12 years of hands-on experience in film and video production. His journey has been shaped by a deep understanding of the creative and practical sides of production—from developing a production plan and coordinating teams to managing locations, logistics and on-ground execution.

Across the years, his experience has extended across TVCs, DVCs, corporate films, brand films, web shows, documentaries and line production, giving him a broad understanding of the demands of different formats and production environments.

For Tushar, every production begins by understanding the story and the objective behind it. From there, the focus is on bringing together the right people, resources, locations and production processes to execute that vision effectively.

As Founder, he works closely with brands, advertising agencies, corporate organizations, creative teams and production partners, helping transform concepts into professionally produced visual content.

With over 12 years of experience in film and video production, Tushar Pancha is the Founder, Director and Executive Producer of Wide View Production—a production house built around a simple belief: great content begins with understanding the story behind the brand.

Tushar brings together creative direction, production expertise and hands-on execution to take a project from concept to completion. He understands that a brand is more than a product or service. That is why his focus is to create content that doesn't merely present a product—it makes the brand feel relevant to the consumer's lifestyle, aspirations and emotions.

Before a camera rolls, Tushar takes the time to understand the brand story, audience, communication objective and desired experience. From developing the creative approach and production strategy to assembling the right team, managing locations, logistics, budgets and on-ground execution, he remains closely involved throughout the entire production journey.

His experience spans TVCs, DVCs, corporate films, brand films, web shows, documentaries and line production, giving him a deep understanding of different formats, production environments and storytelling requirements.

Our role is not simply to showcase what a brand sells. It is to understand why people should care about it, and then translate that understanding into powerful visual storytelling.

From the first conversation to the final frame, Tushar leads the process with a focus on creative thinking, disciplined planning and purposeful execution.
